Our Mission

TDEEBMR exists to make evidence-based nutrition science accessible to everyone. We believe that understanding your body's energy needs should not require an expensive consultation with a dietitian, a subscription to a premium app, or a degree in exercise physiology. Our mission is to provide free, accurate, and transparent calculators that empower individuals to make informed decisions about their nutrition, body composition, and health goals.

We built TDEEBMR because the internet is saturated with nutrition tools that use outdated formulas, proprietary black-box algorithms, or no scientific basis at all. Our calculators are different: every equation we implement is published in peer-reviewed scientific literature, every coefficient is traceable to its original source, and every limitation is clearly disclosed. We treat our users with the intellectual respect they deserve by showing our work.

Our Core Principles

Every calculator on TDEEBMR is built on three principles: (1) use only peer-reviewed, scientifically validated formulas; (2) cite original sources so users can verify our claims; (3) clearly communicate limitations so users understand that these are estimates, not exact measurements.

What We Offer

TDEEBMR provides a suite of interconnected nutrition and body composition calculators, each designed to address a specific aspect of dietary planning:

  • TDEE Calculator: Our flagship tool estimates your Total Daily Energy Expenditure using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation with standardized Physical Activity Level multipliers from FAO/WHO/UNU research.
  • BMR Calculator: Compare three established Basal Metabolic Rate formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor, Harris-Benedict, and Katch-McArdle) side by side.
  • BMI Calculator: Calculate your Body Mass Index with WHO classification categories and health context.
  • Macros Calculator: Get a detailed macronutrient breakdown (protein, carbohydrates, fat) tailored to your goals and activity level.
  • Body Fat Calculator: Estimate body fat percentage using the U.S. Navy circumference method.
  • Ideal Weight Calculator: Compare four clinical ideal body weight formulas (Devine, Robinson, Miller, Hamwi).
  • Calorie Deficit Calculator: Plan a personalized weight loss timeline with safe daily deficit targets.
  • Water Intake Calculator: Estimate daily hydration needs based on body weight, activity, and climate.
  • Protein Calculator: Determine optimal daily protein intake based on ISSN guidelines and activity level.
  • Lean Body Mass Calculator: Calculate lean body mass using multiple established formulas.

In addition to calculators, we publish educational articles on nutrition science topics including TDEE accuracy, metabolic adaptation, activity level guidelines, and the relationship between BMR and TDEE. All educational content follows the same rigorous standards of citation and transparency that govern our calculators.

Our Standards for Accuracy

We hold ourselves to high standards because the information we provide directly influences people's dietary decisions. Here is how we ensure accuracy across everything we publish:

Source Requirements

We only use formulas and data from peer-reviewed scientific journals, official organizational position stands (such as the ISSN, ACSM, and Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics), and established clinical textbooks. We do not rely on blog posts, anecdotal evidence, or unverified sources. Every formula on our site can be traced back to its original publication, and we provide full citations in our Methodology page.

Review Process

All calculator implementations undergo a multi-step review process. First, the formula is coded and tested against published example calculations to verify mathematical accuracy. Second, the Lead Nutritionist reviews the implementation to confirm that it aligns with the intent and constraints described in the original research. Third, the Research Editor verifies that all citations are current and that no newer research has superseded the formula. Finally, the content is reviewed for clarity and accessibility before publication.

Update Policy

Nutrition science evolves continuously. We conduct formal reviews of all formulas and content at least annually, and we perform interim updates when significant new research is published. When we update a calculator, we document the change and the reason for it. Users can always find the most current information about our methods on the Methodology page.

Transparency Commitment

We believe that transparency builds trust. That is why we openly disclose the limitations of every formula we use, the accuracy ranges documented in the research, and the populations for which each formula is most and least appropriate. We do not overstate the precision of our calculators, and we consistently emphasize that these tools provide estimates, not exact measurements.

Our Commitment to E-E-A-T

Google's Search Quality Evaluator Guidelines emphasize four criteria for assessing the quality of health and wellness content: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (E-E-A-T). We take these criteria seriously:

E-E-A-T at TDEEBMR

Experience: Our team has practical, hands-on experience applying nutrition science in real-world settings, from clinical dietetics to athletic performance coaching.
Expertise: All content is created and reviewed by professionals with relevant academic credentials and subject-matter knowledge.
Authoritativeness: We cite primary sources, link to original research, and maintain a transparent methodology that can be independently verified.
Trustworthiness: We clearly disclose limitations, avoid sensational claims, provide a comprehensive medical disclaimer, and maintain editorial independence from any commercial interests.

Privacy and Data Practices

We respect your privacy. All calculations on TDEEBMR are performed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No personal data, health information, or calculation inputs are transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere. You can review our complete data practices in our Privacy Policy.
