We value feedback from our users and are committed to maintaining the accuracy and quality of our calculators and content. Whether you have found an error in a formula, want to suggest a new calculator, have a partnership inquiry, or simply want to say hello, please reach out using the information below.

Get in Touch

Email Us

General inquiries: info@tdeebmr.com
Privacy concerns: privacy@tdeebmr.com
Legal matters: legal@tdeebmr.com

Response Time

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 48 hours during business days. Complex questions regarding our methodology, formula implementations, or scientific references may take up to 5 business days for a thorough response. If you do not receive a reply within this timeframe, please follow up as your message may have been missed.

Send Us a Message

You can also use the form below to send us a message directly. Please fill in all required fields and provide as much detail as possible so we can assist you effectively.

We Read Every Message

Every inquiry sent to TDEEBMR is read by a member of our team. We take user feedback seriously and use it to improve our calculators, correct errors, and develop new tools. If you have found a discrepancy in one of our formulas, please include the specific calculator, the input values you used, the result you received, and what you believe the correct result should be. This helps us investigate and resolve issues quickly.
