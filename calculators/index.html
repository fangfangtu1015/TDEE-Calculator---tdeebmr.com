How to Use These Calculators

Each calculator on TDEEBMR is built on peer-reviewed research and established clinical formulas. All computations run locally in your browser, meaning your personal data never leaves your device.

For the most accurate results, we recommend:

  • Measure your weight first thing in the morning after using the bathroom
  • Use a calibrated scale and stadiometer for height measurements
  • For body circumference methods, measure at the same time of day consistently
  • Be honest about your activity level; most people overestimate it
  • Use the results as starting points and adjust based on 2-3 weeks of tracked outcomes

Our methodology page provides full citations for every formula used across these calculators.

This tool provides estimates for educational purposes only. Consult a healthcare professional before making dietary changes.
